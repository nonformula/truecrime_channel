09 — Brand Rules

Do & Don't

Do

Use dark backgrounds exclusively. The site is a dark experience — #0a0a0a as the canvas.

Don't

Add light-mode sections, white backgrounds, or light cards in the flow of the dark site.

Do

Use red (#d32127) exclusively for emphasis and callout accents within copy — in <em> tags or callout left borders.

Don't

Use red for buttons, backgrounds, headers, or any structural element. It's a punctuation color only.

Do

Apply negative letter-spacing to all large Inter headings (−0.02em to −0.035em). This is what gives headings their sharp, editorial quality.

Don't

Use default or positive letter-spacing on display or subheading type. It reads amateurish at large sizes.

Do

Keep card gaps at 3px. This tight gutter is a signature of the design — it creates the film-strip / editorial grid feel.

Don't

Increase card gaps to 8px, 16px, or more for "breathing room." The tightness is intentional.

Do

Use clamp() for all typography and spacing to achieve fluid scaling between mobile and desktop.

Don't

Use fixed pixel sizes for display text or section padding. This creates harsh jumps at breakpoints.

Do

Use Roboto Mono 700 uppercase with generous tracking (0.14–0.22em) only for labels, nav links, and metadata. It identifies organizational / structural text.

Don't

Use Roboto Mono for body copy, headings, or anything longer than a short label. It's a labeling typeface, not a reading typeface.

Do

Ensure every video has a poster frame fallback image. The site uses .mov which is unsupported on Android — graceful degradation to a static poster is required.

Don't

Ship a video without a poster attribute. A blank black rectangle on Android is a broken experience.

Do

Place image grids on the left in split layouts, text on the right. This is the established visual rhythm of the site.

Don't

Reverse the split layout without strong reason — it breaks the visual rhythm established across sections.
