# DARKHORSE · What the Audience Sees

Oct 13, 2026 · @AC

Every beat uses only four kinds of picture: public-record footage (police body cameras, court evidence, police interviews), maps, first-person reconstructions, and our own graphics. No news, archival or personal photos.

## Cold open · The Night

1. **The call.** Black screen. A woman's voice on a 911 recording: she has just walked into her apartment and her friend is on the bathroom floor, and there is blood everywhere. A white title types on: Austin, Texas · May 11, 2022 · 9:54 PM.
2. **The stairs.** Police body-camera footage at night. Flashlights swing up a set of outside stairs to a small apartment above a garage. Inside, the camera stays on the officers and the floor; we never see Mo. An officer points out a shell casing by his knee, then another, and counts three. The narrator tells us who is on that floor: Mo Wilson, 25, one of the best gravel racers in the country. She had been in Austin for less than two days.
3. **Her face.** A trial photograph of Mo, smiling, holds on screen in silence for a beat. The narrator: she had come here for a race that weekend.
4. **The bike.** Body-camera footage in the dark yard and alley. Officers ask where her bike should be; it isn't by the door. A flashlight finds it down the alley, hidden in a stand of bamboo. The narrator: whoever did this took the time to hide it.
5. **A name.** Body-camera footage at the foot of the stairs. Her friend, shaken, tells an officer Mo spent the evening swimming with her friend Colin.
6. **The next day.** Hard cut to a police interview room, filmed from a fixed ceiling camera. A woman in her thirties sits very still. A detective tells her the warrant that brought her in has the wrong birth date, so she is free to go. She asks to leave. He asks why her vehicle was next to that apartment the night before. She asks again to leave. The narrator: tomorrow she sells that vehicle, and the day after she is on a plane. A two-column panel slides in: what police know, and what we know. Title.

## Chapter 1 · Mo

7. **Who she was.** The two trial photos of Mo sit side by side, then give way to text cards carrying her family's words from the courtroom: a small town in Vermont, skiing and riding as a kid, ski racing at Dartmouth, two knee injuries, and the day she quit her job to race full time. Her father's card: she told him it just felt so right.
8. **The morning of May 11.** A labeled reconstruction from Mo's point of view, like a chest-mounted action camera. A morning ride through Austin: handlebars, gloved hands, bright sun. A small activity-tracker readout in the corner. Just a good morning on a bike, hours before anything happens.
9. **Why Austin.** An animated map draws a line from San Francisco to Austin, then a short line north to the small Texas town where she's racing that weekend.
10. **Who's who.** An animated chart builds: Mo, a pro cyclist named Colin, and his partner Kaitlin. A short break-up in fall 2021 is marked on a timeline, and a line briefly links Colin and Mo before he goes back to Kaitlin.
11. **A text in January.** A graphic phone screen shows a message Mo sent Colin in January: the weekend was strange for her, and she wants to know where things stand. His reply appears below it.
12. **What police don't know yet.** Quote cards. A friend of Kaitlin's remembers her saying she would kill a new girlfriend of Colin's. Another remembers her talking about getting a gun. The viewer column fills with its first entry.

## Chapter 2 · The Last Evening

13. **The lock log.** The actual door-lock record entered as evidence fills the screen. Two entries light up: locked at 5:55 PM, unlocked with Mo's personal code at 8:36 PM.
14. **The evening.** A short labeled reconstruction from Mo's point of view: late sun on an outdoor pool, her hands on the pool edge. Then an animated map of Austin. A dot runs from the apartment to the pool by the river, then to a burger stand next door. A clock runs forward and stops at 8:16 PM as the dot heads back east.
15. **One minute apart.** A minute-by-minute timeline. 8:35, the motorcycle passes north through the alley. 8:36, the door code. Same minute, a text card from Colin to Kaitlin: his phone died, he's heading home. The police column updates.
16. **The neighbor's camera.** Security-camera footage entered as evidence: a driveway at night. A dark SUV with a bike rack on the back and a rack on the roof rolls in, slows, and stops beside the house. The timestamp reads 8:37 PM. The police column updates.
17. **Silence.** Black. The narrator says only that Mo's phone call ended at 9:13, and that a little after 9:15 a neighbor's camera picked up sound. We hear nothing. Three seconds of silence, then the 911 title card from the opening returns: 9:54 PM.

## Chapter 3 · The Gun

18. **The SUV in the driveway.** Side by side: a frame from the neighbor's camera and police evidence photos of a black 2012 Jeep. Callouts match the rear bike rack, the roof rack and the chrome window trim.
19. **Colin's interview.** Police interview footage the next day. Colin talks openly and at length: the pool, the burgers, dropping Mo off, riding home.
20. **The gun.** Trial evidence photos: a compact 9mm handgun found when police searched the house Colin shared with Kaitlin, then the shell casings from the apartment, each with its evidence marker. Under them, an officer's body-camera audio counts the casings. The police column updates: Kaitlin's gun was in the house.
21. **Back in the room.** The interview room from the opening, now split-screen with Colin's. He talks; she sits motionless, offers no explanation for the Jeep, and asks to leave.
22. **The deleted map.** A graphic of a saved map pin on a phone screen disappearing, dated May 12. The narrator: that day she deleted a map showing where Mo was staying and changed her email address. The viewer column updates.

## Chapter 4 · The Sale

23. **The sale.** Dealership security footage entered as evidence: the showroom floor and service desk on the evening of May 13. A graphic card gives the price, $12,200. The viewer column updates.
24. **The anonymous call.** An audio waveform on a dark screen as we hear part of a recorded call to police from a woman who won't give her name. She says Kaitlin told her in January she was angry enough to kill Mo.
25. **Two flights.** A day counter appears. An animated map draws a flight line from Austin to Houston, then on to New York.
26. **The match.** The arrest warrant affidavit fills the screen, key lines highlighted one at a time: the lab test on her gun, the link to the casings, the charge of murder. The police column catches up by one.
27. **The passport.** A labeled reconstruction from her point of view: a departure gate, her hands holding a passport and boarding pass, nothing readable. The narrator: the passport is her sister's. The viewer column updates.

## Chapter 5 · The Ad

28. **Costa Rica.** The map pulls south to Costa Rica and settles on the Pacific coast. Graphic cards stack up: new names, dyed and cut hair, surgery on her nose. A graphic search bar types the kinds of searches she made, including one about burning off fingerprints.
29. **The trap.** A labeled graphic recreation of an online ad: a beach hostel looking for a yoga instructor as soon as possible. A week ticks by on screen with no reply. Then a new email appears.
30. **The meeting.** A labeled reconstruction from her point of view: a sandy path toward a beach hostel in afternoon light, the entrance getting closer. Hard cut to silence and black, then her booking photo. The two columns of the side panel finally match, and the panel closes.

## Close

31. **The run.** A map of South Austin. A line traces about a mile from a doctor's office to where deputies caught her when she tried to escape, weeks before trial.
32. **The verdict.** The evidence from the film lines up across the screen as cards: the door code, the camera, the gun, the sale, the passport, the ad. A date appears, November 16, 2023, with one word: guilty. The next card reads 90 years.
33. **The family.** A slow map move north and east to Vermont. Text cards carry her family's words from the courtroom, including her brother's memory of the two of them texting each other the three best things from their day.
34. **Mo.** A labeled reconstruction from Mo's point of view: riding a forest trail in Vermont in golden light. It fades to her trial photo, smiling. The film ends on her.
