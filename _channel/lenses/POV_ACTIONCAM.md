# Lens: POV_ACTIONCAM

**Use for:** an active, outdoorsy subject whose world is movement (cyclist, runner, climber). Usually the victim's lens: alive, kinetic, life-affirming.

**Frame:** ultra-wide action cam, slight barrel distortion, chest or handlebar mount, natural motion and micro-shake, wearer's hands/arms/equipment may enter frame. Wearer's face never seen.

**Prompt fragment:**
> first-person POV from a chest-mounted action camera, ultra-wide lens with slight barrel distortion, natural handheld micro-shake, bright natural daylight, shallow motion blur at frame edges, hands and forearms occasionally visible, no faces, documentary realism

**Negative:** visible face of the wearer, readable text or signage, police HUD, timestamp burn-ins, cinematic lens flares, slow-motion

**Grade:** warm, slightly lifted blacks, high clarity, light grain.

**HUD (applied in After Effects, never generated):** activity-tracker overlay. Speed, heart rate, GPS lat/lon, elevation, clock. Bottom-left: `RECONSTRUCTION · SOURCE: <citation>`. Values come from the shot spec, never invented. If real data isn't known, show only clock and source.
