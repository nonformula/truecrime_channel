# Lens: WIDE_RECON

**Use for:** non-POV establishing shots between chapters, places and objects (exteriors, a vehicle in a lot, an empty room, a document on a table). Gives the viewer a break from first-person.

**Frame:** locked-off or slow push, 35–50mm equivalent, environment and objects only. No identifiable people; silhouettes at distance only if documented.

**Prompt fragment:**
> locked-off documentary establishing shot, 35mm lens, natural available light for the documented time and weather, environment and objects only, no identifiable people, restrained composition, photographic realism

**Negative:** identifiable faces, readable text, crime-scene depiction, dramatic stylization

**Grade:** channel neutral, consistent across cases.

**HUD (After Effects):** `RECONSTRUCTION · SOURCE: <citation>` only.
