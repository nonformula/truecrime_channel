# Lens: POV_DASH

**Use for:** documented vehicle movement (approach, departure, route) when no real dashcam exists.

**Frame:** fixed windshield mount, wide lens, steering wheel edge or hood at frame bottom, road ahead. Driver never seen.

**Prompt fragment:**
> view from a windshield-mounted camera inside a moving car, wide lens, hood edge at bottom of frame, road ahead, natural light for the documented time of day, steady with road vibration, no faces, no readable plates or signs

**Negative:** driver visible, readable license plates, readable street signs, police dashcam overlay

**Grade:** neutral, slight compression, light grain.

**HUD (After Effects):** clock, route name from the map spec. `RECONSTRUCTION · SOURCE: <citation>`. Must look clearly different from any real police dashcam in the episode.
