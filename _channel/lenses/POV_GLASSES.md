# Lens: POV_GLASSES

**Use for:** a subject whose documented actions are deliberate or concealed. Usually the suspect's lens: cold, observed, clinical.

**Frame:** eye-level, normal-to-slightly-wide lens, steady head motion, subtle vignette, narrower framing than the action cam. Wearer's hands may appear for documented actions only. Wearer's face never seen (no mirrors, no reflections).

**Prompt fragment:**
> first-person POV at eye level from smart glasses, natural field of view, steady subtle head movement, muted cool color, soft vignette, documentary realism, hands visible only when performing the action, no faces, no reflections of the wearer

**Negative:** mirrors or reflective surfaces showing the wearer, faces, readable text, police bodycam look, dramatic lighting

**Grade:** cool, desaturated, crushed highlights, fine grain.

**HUD (After Effects):** minimal. Clock, date, location name. Bottom-left: `RECONSTRUCTION · SOURCE: <citation>`.
