# Channel Bible

Living document. Tune it after the pilot (C001).

## Lane

VO-driven, evidence-led true crime. An omniscient human narrator walks the viewer through a real case using primary-source footage (bodycam, interrogation, surveillance, 911, news), Swiss-disciplined motion graphics, and clearly labeled AI recreations for moments no camera captured.

## Format

Every episode uses **Chain of Custody** (`format_chain_of_custody.md`): cold open on dramatic irony, chapters as exhibits, the Knowledge Ledger, a baseline beat where footage allows, close on the victim.

## Look

- Typography: Helvetica Neue, The Seasons for select titling. Strict grid, flat color, no rounded corners or drop shadows.
- Accent red for ledger, exhibit tags and map markers (`#E0262B`, matches `map_styles.yaml`).
- Recreations have their own grade per lens (see `lenses/`) and always carry the RECONSTRUCTION tag.

## Do

- Let real footage carry emotion. Narrator sets up, footage pays off, narrator interprets.
- Re-hook every 5 to 8 minutes with an open question.
- Give the victim a voice early (their own clips, words, life).
- Disclose every recreation on screen, in the description, and with YouTube's altered/synthetic content setting.

## Don't

- Sensationalize, speculate, or editorialize guilt beyond the verdict and record.
- Invent dialogue, thoughts or events.
- Show the crime or a victim's final moments, in footage or recreation.
- Use photoreal AI faces of real people.
- Show private residences at street level or plot uninvolved people.
- Publish temp AI VO.

## Runtime

40 to 50 minutes. Chapters map to YouTube chapters. Recreation-led moments get cut for Shorts after publish.
