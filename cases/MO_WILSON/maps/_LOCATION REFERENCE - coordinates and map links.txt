MAP BUILD NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Three layers worth building:

1. AUSTIN BLOCK LEVEL - pool, restaurant, apartment, Strickland residence.
   The point is how small the geography was. Needs the two missing addresses.

2. THE FLIGHT - Austin, upstate NY, Newark, San Jose. Passport is the hinge.

3. COSTA RICA TIMING - San Jose, Jaco, Santa Teresa. The strongest graphic
   available: the marshals were searching Santa Teresa while she was back in
   San Jose having surgery. They missed each other in time, not in space.
   No existing coverage has visualized this.

For production maps use Mapbox Studio (custom styling, clean commercial license)
or Google Earth Studio for the animated flight path. Google Maps screengrabs are
not cleared for monetized video.
